ASSET AUDIT AND DECISIONS
-------------------------
- Inspected all supplied Boss Animation, Boss Accessories, Boss Weapons and
  One-Eye archives, including FBX structure, skeleton, mesh and clip metadata.
- The animation packs use the standard Mixamo hierarchy at 30 FPS. Twenty-eight
  representative files were converted without authoring changes and catalogued
  in public/assets/boss_v10_25/animation_manifest.json with source filename,
  trim range, category and legacy fallback.
- The production boss rig is Root -> Hip with Waist/Spine01/Spine02 torso,
  L_/R_ limb naming and a validated skinned body/dress/hair. Retargeting uses
  semantic role aliases and compensates for unmapped intermediary target bones.
- Crown, Witch Cuff, Moon Choker and Nocturne Core were static mesh assets. They
  are attached through controlled scene anchors so they follow their bones but
  never become accidental Skeleton children or break bind-pose matrices.
- The supplied long skirt is a static non-skinned mesh. It was deliberately not
  rigid-bound to the hips because that would fail the no-detachment/no-float
  acceptance criterion; the proven skinned production dress remains active.
- The supplied One-Eye asset is a static single-mesh model. It is used as the
  creature body while procedural eye/tentacle layers provide readable hover,
  aim, pulse, charge, lunge and death motion.

ANIMATION PIPELINE
------------------
- BossRetargeter captures source and target rest-world quaternions before play.
- Every clip is sampled at 30 FPS after per-clip trim.
- Target world rotation is composed from target rest, inverse source rest and
  source animated rotation, then converted back through the actual target
  parent chain. This avoids direct local-quaternion copying.
- Only rotations and clamped vertical hips translation are transferred.
  Horizontal root/hips motion and all scale tracks are excluded.
- Full-body, upper-body and lower-body masks are registered. Magic aim is a
  restrained additive chain through hips/spine/chest/neck/head.
- Representative clips load first; all failures fall back per manifest and are
  exposed in telemetry rather than blocking the match.

COMBAT PIPELINE
---------------
- Logical actions define animation, fallback, category, startup, impact,
  recovery, blend, aim, layers, Orb/Halo state, effect and server trajectory.
- Twelve Combo Graph families replace flat random sequencing:
  Lunar Fang, Witch Hunt, Eclipse Breaker, Phantom Waltz,
  Crescent Reversal, Gravity Execution, Moonstep Barrage, False Opening,
  Broken Rhythm, Eclipse Chase, Moon Guillotine and Black Hole Ballet.
- Branches re-evaluate live context at each node. Weighted selection considers
  phase, range, recent dodge/perfect-dodge direction, aggression, defense,
  melee/ranged preference, cooldowns and recent combo history.
- Movement clips never drive authoritative displacement. Dash, dodge, chase,
  lunge and teleport positions are computed on the server with bounded arena
  destinations and replicated as trajectory/teleport events.
- Perfect Parry uses the existing sword counter window against active melee,
  forces a designed stagger and uses the strongest 145 ms impact tier. Existing
  Super Armor, Poise, weak points and Critical Break rules remain intact.

ORB, HALO, MOBS AND ULTIMATE
----------------------------
- Orb states: FOLLOW, ORBIT, AUTONOMOUS, CHARGE, PROJECTILE, FREE_FLOAT, TRAP,
  RECALL and ULTIMATE.
- Halo states: IDLE, QUICK_CAST, HEAVY_CAST, VORTEX, TELEPORT,
  PHASE_TRANSITION, ULTIMATE, COLLAPSE and CRITICAL_BREAK.
- Phase 2/3 support director spawns at most three One-Eye mobs and coordinates
  their Void Bolt, Gaze Beam and Abyss Lunge choices with the boss action cue.
- Eternal Eclipse · Zero Hour runs eight authoritative scheduled stages:
  Time Dilation, Telegraph Field, Phantom Casters, Orb Sky Array,
  Eclipse Slam, Starfall, Final Collapse and Recovery.
- Time dilation affects client animation/camera presentation only. Damage,
  hazards, hit validation and game time remain server-authoritative.

  blend/layer state, Combo Graph/node/branch, distance, dodge memory, Poise,
