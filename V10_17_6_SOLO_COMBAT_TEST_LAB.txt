PRINCESS RESCUE V10.17.6 — SOLO COMBAT TEST LAB

HOW TO USE
==========
1. From the home screen, choose `SOLO COMBAT TEST — BẤT TỬ`.
2. Wait until the accepted Tripo boss reports ready.
3. Choose `BẮT ĐẦU SOLO TEST`; no Princess device is required.
4. During combat, Hero and Princess HP stay at 100 while boss HP decreases.
5. Use Capture / Download Debug ZIP as usual when a current visual anomaly occurs.

SAFETY BOUNDARY
===============
- Invulnerability is authorized by the server only for rooms carrying the
  exact `boss-only-damage` test-mode flag.
- Normal co-op rooms keep their existing damage, down and revive behavior.
- Boss AI, skills, damage output, hitboxes, animations, VFX and 2200 HP remain
  active so the combat test is representative.
- The UI displays a green test badge throughout the test session.
