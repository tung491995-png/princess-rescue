PRINCESS RESCUE V10.17.1 — INTRO STABILITY HOTFIX

ROOT CAUSES FIXED
- updateCam previously pulled toward an animated target and then
  frameBossFullBody pushed away again every frame. This feedback loop caused
  visible camera oscillation around the dancing silhouette.
- The mobile framebuffer was resized at intro start/end; browser-chrome resize
  events could resize it again during the orbit.
- Boss Y bob and snapshot interpolation continued underneath the cinematic.
- Rune and black-moon materials first compiled only when made visible.
- A capped 33 ms mixer step could make animation clips lag the real-time intro.

V10.17.1 BEHAVIOR
- Deterministic four-phase camera path with continuous boundaries.
- Fixed portrait/landscape camera radii; full boss, halo and crown stay framed.
- Intro boss root locked to v108IntroAnchor.
- No intro camera trauma, camera kick, hit-stop or dynamic framebuffer changes.
- Resize is deferred until 40 ms after the 10.5 s cinematic.
- Intro VFX WebGL programs are prewarmed behind the lobby.
- Cinematic animation uses raw frame delta capped by the existing 80 ms guard.
