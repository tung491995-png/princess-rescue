PRINCESS RESCUE V10.15 — ORB WEAPON & HALO FOUNDATION

FOUNDATION
==========
- Optimized mystical orb GLB follows a dedicated LeftHandOrbSocket.
- The orb remains a world-space prop so animation scale and hand-roll cannot deform it.
- A small torso-separation guard keeps the orb near the palm without intersecting the boss body.
- Ornamental halo is a scene-level follower behind the boss, independent from skeleton and hitbox.
- Orb/halo are loaded and precompiled before the multiplayer boss-ready packet is sent.

MOBILE ASSETS
=============
- Orb: 1,894,640 -> 31,387 triangles; 58 MB -> ~1.2 MB; three 1K textures.
- Halo: 14,160 triangles; ~1.6 MB; three 1K textures.

ANIMATION AUDIT
===============
The `?visualaudit=1` route samples all 19 clips at 0%, 20%, 50%, 80% and 98%.
Every sample checks Tripo visibility, finite prop transforms, orb socket drift,
distance from the left hand, halo root following and ROOT XZ hitbox lock.
