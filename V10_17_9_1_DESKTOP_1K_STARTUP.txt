PRINCESS RESCUE V10.17.9.1 — DESKTOP 1K STARTUP

- Desktop and mobile both use the 1K Tripo boss in the critical startup path.
- The desktop startup candidate list no longer contains the 2K boss.
- The active match never parses, compiles or swaps to the 2K model.
- After 20 seconds, the 2K file may download at low priority only when:
  the intro is over, the boss/orb/halo are ready, FPS is stable, the tab is
  visible and Data Saver / 2G is not active.
- The 2K response is consumed as a Blob so the browser can retain its immutable
  HTTP cache entry without constructing another Three.js model.
- V10.17.9 parallel boss/orb/halo startup, 512px props and all combat/debug
  behavior remain unchanged.
